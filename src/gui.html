About Gesture Manager

Laptop Touchpad Gesture Manager & Remapper is an ultra-lightweight, zero-bloat cross-platform daemon written in Rust. It captures raw Precision Touchpad (PTP) packets on Windows and libinput/udev event streams on Linux, converting multi-touch contacts into synthesized shortcuts and scripts.
